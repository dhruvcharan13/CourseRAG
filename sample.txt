Lecture 6 — Balanced Binary Search Trees

An AVL tree is a self-balancing binary search tree where the heights of the two
child subtrees of any node differ by at most one. Lookups, insertions, and
deletions all take O(log n) time in the worst case.

Rotations (left, right, left-right, right-left) restore the balance invariant
after an insertion or deletion changes a subtree's height.
